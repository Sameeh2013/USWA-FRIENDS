Our Story

The USWA FRIENDS batch of 2024 was formed at Darul Huda Islamic University, where a group of dedicated students came together with a shared vision of Islamic education and lifelong friendship.

Our batch name "USWA" means "example" in Arabic, reflecting our commitment to following the example of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) in all aspects of life. We spent years studying Islamic sciences, Arabic language, and modern education while building bonds that would last a lifetime.

Today, our members serve as scholars, educators, community leaders, and professionals across the globe, carrying forward the knowledge and values instilled during our formative years at Darul Huda.
